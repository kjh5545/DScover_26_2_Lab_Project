> A 58-year-old woman with a 9-year history of type 2 diabetes reports fatigue and increased thirst; her HbA1c remains above target despite metformin. She has taken metformin 1000 mg twice daily at the same dose for the past two years and has never used any other glucose-lowering medication, including SGLT2 inhibitors, DPP-4 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists, or insulin. Her HbA1c measured at her clinic last week was 9.8%, and her fasting blood glucose was 11.2 mmol/L. Her BMI is 31.2 kg/m², her blood pressure is 138/84 mmHg on lisinopril, and her eGFR is 78 mL/min/1.73 m². She also takes levothyroxine for hypothyroidism at an unchanged dose for the past two years. An eye examination three months ago showed mild nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy that does not need treatment. She has no history of pancreatitis, heart attack, or stroke. She is postmenopausal and works as an accountant.
